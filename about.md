Mouhcine Guettabi is the Ronald and Cynthia McNeill Distinguished Professor in Business, Professor of Economics, and Regional Economist at the University of North Carolina Wilmington. He tracks the region's economic indicators, produces economic impact analyses and forecasts, and regularly briefs local businesses, media, and policymakers on the state of the economy.

## Research interests

- Universal basic income and cash transfer programs
- Regional and local labor market dynamics
- Health economics, including healthcare spending and utilization
- Natural resource economics and extractive industries
- Applied microeconomics using quasi-experimental methods

## Selected background

Mouhcine joined UNCW in 2021. Before that, he spent nearly a decade at the University of Alaska Anchorage, where he was an associate professor of economics and a faculty member at the Institute of Social and Economic Research, producing state economic forecasts and testifying before the Alaska legislature. He earned his PhD in economics from Oklahoma State University in 2012, with a focus on urban and regional economics.

His academic work has appeared in the Review of Economics and Statistics, the Journal of Health Economics, the Journal of Environmental Economics and Management, the Journal of Policy Analysis and Management, and the Journal of Regional Science, among others. A full list of his published research is on the [Research](/research/) page.

## A note on this site

This site is a place to share three kinds of work: applied research on North Carolina and the surrounding region, academic papers published in peer-reviewed journals, and shorter, less formal updates on topics I find interesting. The goal is to keep all of it in one place, easy to find and easy to link to.
